Glossary & Methodology
How This Model Works
Three independent model generations — V1, V2, and V3 — each run LR + LGBM pairs to predict home win probability for every MLB game.

V1: LR (C=0.5) + LGBM · 36 features · trained through 2022
V2: LR (C=0.001) + LGBM · 35 features (pruned from 98) · trained through 2021
V3: LR (C=0.001) + LGBM · 35 features · walk-forward retrained through 2025

Display probability: Vegas-anchored calibration (Session 21) — logit(p) = a + b·logit(vegas devig), fit on 2023-25 verified odds. The best Brier score available: beats raw Vegas OOS (2025: 0.24281 vs 0.24473; live 2026: 0.24687 vs 0.24739). The 6-blend (equal weight V1/V2/V3 LR+LGBM) still runs underneath: it drives all Pick flags, edges, and bet tiers, and is shown on flagged bet cards as "Model Prob". All-games CLV and the calibration table track the displayed (anchored) probability going forward.

The three generations use different feature sets, regularization, and training windows — making them genuinely independent. Multi-model agreement dramatically outperforms single-model flags. All performance figures below use modal de-vigged odds computed consistently across all tiers.

Odds validation: All backtest ROI figures use verified pre-game odds from The Odds API historical endpoint. Best available line across sportsbooks, matching the line-shopping approach used for live betting.

Dashboard Terms
Model (games table)
Calibrated win probability for the pick side — the Vegas-anchored recalibration, the most accurate single number available (beats raw Vegas Brier OOS). The bell curve beside it shows the spread of the six sub-models. All numbers in the table are shown from the pick side's perspective.
Model Prob (flagged cards)
Win probability averaged across all six sub-models (V1/V2/V3 LR+LGBM). Shown on flagged bet cards because the tier edge math is computed from these sub-models — the adjacent "Calibrated" cell gives the anchored number for comparison.
Edge (games table)
Calibrated Model minus Vegas for the pick side. Signed and small by construction — the calibrated probability rarely strays far from the market. Tier flags do NOT come from this number; they come from sub-model agreement (see tier rules below).

Confirming & Warning Signals — Away Bets
Green and orange badges appear on flagged away bets based on game-context patterns. These don't change the bet recommendation — they indicate when conditions are especially favorable or unfavorable. Stats are validated on the actual production tier definitions using modal de-vigged odds (OOS 2023-2025). "All Away" = STRONG + PRIME + STANDARD combined (191 bets).
Game 3+ of series
Edge is dramatically stronger later in a series, likely driven by compounding bullpen fatigue and lineup familiarity. OOS: STRONG +73.3% ROI (33 bets, 76% win), All Away +60.1% (62 bets, 69% win). The single strongest confirming signal.
Disciplined opponent
Home lineup has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). Counterintuitively, edge is strongest against disciplined teams. OOS: STRONG +68.8% ROI (50 bets, 68% win), All Away +52.5% (95 bets, 62% win).
Fatigued opposing bullpen
Home bullpen threw more pitches in prior 2 days (2d pitch gap > 0). OOS: STRONG +67.7% ROI (58 bets, 67% win) vs +3.7% when not fatigued. All Away +50.4% (116 bets, 62% win) vs +7.3%.
Game 2 caution
Game 2 of a series is the weakest spot. OOS: STRONG -11.3% ROI (33 bets, 36% win), All Away -2.8% (60 bets, 42% win). Negative ROI — exercise caution or skip.
Undisciplined opponent
Home lineup has above-median chase rate (less disciplined). OOS: STRONG +12.7% ROI (51 bets, 49% win), All Away +14.7% (96 bets, 51% win). Still profitable but roughly a third of the disciplined-opponent ROI.
September
September is the weakest month, likely due to roster expansion and callups. OOS: STRONG +9.1% ROI (13 bets, small sample), All Away +30.9% (38 bets, 55% win). Still positive but weaker edge — use caution.
Confirming & Warning Signals — Home Bets
Home bets (Narrow Edge 365 bets, Home Edge 432 bets) have different patterns than away bets. Stats are validated on the actual production tier definitions using modal de-vigged odds (OOS 2023-2025). Note: Narrow Edge already requires a fatigue/park gate, so fatigue and park splits within Narrow Edge are less informative — Home Edge stats are more meaningful for those signals.
HR-prone opposing starter
Away starter has above-median HR/FB rate — more vulnerable to home run power. Narrow Edge OOS: +18.9% ROI (182 bets, 57% win) vs +5.1% when low HR/FB. Home Edge: +13.8% (216 bets, 56% win) vs +10.6%.
Games 1-2 of series
Unlike away bets, home bets perform better early in a series. Narrow Edge OOS: +17.1% ROI (217 bets, 56% win) vs +4.4% in Game 3+. Home Edge: +13.3% (254 bets, 56% win) vs +10.6%.
Disciplined home lineup
Home team has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). This signal transfers from away bets. Narrow Edge OOS: +14.6% ROI (182 bets, 57% win) vs +9.3% when undisciplined.
Fatigued opposing bullpen
For Home Edge (which has no fatigue gate), opposing bullpen fatigue (1d gap > 0) provides a modest lift. Home Edge OOS: +14.6% ROI (200 bets, 56% win) vs +10.1% when not fatigued. For Narrow Edge, the fatigue gate is already a prerequisite.
Game 3+ (weaker for home bets)
Unlike away bets where Game 3+ is the strongest spot, home bets weaken later in a series. Narrow Edge OOS: +4.4% ROI (148 bets, 49% win) vs +17.1% in Games 1-2. The pattern is inverted from away.
September
September is weaker for both home and away bets. Narrow Edge OOS: +5.3% ROI (80 bets, 49% win) vs +13.8% non-September. Home Edge: +7.2% (95 bets, 54% win) vs +13.6%. Still positive but thinner edge.
Roster sensitivity: Home bets are more sensitive to lineup changes than away bets. The dashboard requires at least "projected" roster confidence — "starter only" or "unknown" rosters will not trigger home flags. When projected, verify the lineup before placing.
Batting & Lineup Stats
wRC+
Weighted Runs Created Plus. Total offensive value adjusted for park and league. 100 = average; 120 = 20% above average.
wOBA
Weighted On-Base Average. Weights each outcome (single, double, HR, walk) by its actual run value. League average ~.310-.320.
Chase Rate
Percentage of pitches outside the strike zone a batter swings at. Lower = more disciplined.
Lineup K Rate
How often a team's batters strike out. Lower = more contact, more balls in play.
Top 3 wOBA
Combined wOBA of a team's top 3 lineup spots — the strength of the heart of the order.
Pitching Stats
xFIP
Expected Fielding Independent Pitching. Estimates run prevention independent of defense and batted-ball luck. Uses expected HR rate. Lower = better. League avg ~4.00.
ERA
Earned Run Average. Earned runs allowed per 9 innings. Lower = better. More volatile than xFIP because it includes defense and sequencing.
Stuff+
Pitch Stuff Plus. Raw quality of a pitcher's arsenal — velocity, movement, spin. 100 = average; 120+ = elite. Since June 2026, computed by an in-house model (v6a) distilled from Statcast pitch physics after FanGraphs access ended — leave-one-year-out correlation 0.85 with the original FG metric.
K Rate
Percentage of plate appearances ending in a strikeout. Higher = more dominant pitching.
Bullpen ERA
Collective ERA of a team's relief pitchers. Matters late in games when starters exit.
Team & Context Stats
Pythagorean Win%
Expected win percentage based on runs scored vs allowed over the last 60 days. Captures true team strength better than W-L record.
Run Differential
Runs scored minus runs allowed (60-day window). Positive = outscoring opponents.
Park Factor
How much a ballpark inflates or deflates run scoring. 1.00 = neutral; Coors Field ~1.30 (30% more runs).
Betting Terms
Edge
Gap between a model probability and Vegas implied probability for the pick side. In the games table this is the calibrated edge (signed, small); tier rules use sub-model edges (de-vigged, per generation).
ROI
Return on Investment. Profit / total risked. +20% means $20 profit per $100 wagered.
Units (u)
Standardized bet size. 1u = your chosen base wager. Flat staking per tier: production tiers 1u (NARROW EDGE 0.5u; unconfirmed lineups open at 0.25u); V4 profiles 0.5u.
Moneyline
Negative = risk that much to win $100 (-150 = risk $150 to win $100). Positive = profit on $100 bet (+130 = $130 profit).
Closing Line Value
Difference between the odds you bet and the final odds at game time. Consistently beating the close is the strongest sign of a profitable bettor.
OOS
Out-of-Sample. Data the model never saw during training. Our models train on 2001-2022; seasons 2023-2025 are OOS and represent real predictive performance.
