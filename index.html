Glossary & Methodology
How This Model Works
Two independent model generations — V1 and V2 — each run their own LR + LGBM pair to predict home win probability for every MLB game.

V1 uses 36 features (lineup quality, pitching matchups, recent form, park factor, etc.), trained on 50,867 games through 2022, with moderate regularization (C=0.5).
V2 expanded the feature pipeline to 98 candidates — adding travel distance, bullpen fatigue, platoon splits, and advanced pitching metrics — then pruned to the best 35 via walk-forward importance ranking. V2 uses a slightly earlier training cutoff (through 2021) and heavier regularization (C=0.001). V2's LR and LGBM sub-models are checked individually for consensus — the original 75/25 blend is not used (analysis showed it adds no predictive value beyond individual sub-model checks).

The two generations use different feature sets, different regularization, and different training windows — making them genuinely independent. The STRONG / PRIME / STANDARD tiers fire only when both V1 and V2 independently flag the same game on the same side. In out-of-sample testing, consensus bets dramatically outperform single-model flags: V1+V2 agreement produces +26-41% verified ROI while single-model-only flags are essentially breakeven or negative. All bets are flat 1-unit stakes.

Odds validation: All backtest ROI figures use verified pre-game odds pulled ~2 hours before first pitch from The Odds API historical endpoint — the same timing window the live dashboard operates on. This means the backtest reflects realistic, bettable lines you would actually see when placing wagers, not stale or post-game prices. Odds were compared across 15-20 sportsbooks per game to find the best available line, matching the line-shopping approach recommended for live betting.
STRONG — Highest Conviction
Rule: All 4 sub-models (V1 LR, V1 LGBM, V2 LR, V2 LGBM) independently agree on away edge ≥12%. This is the most restrictive filter — every model must clear the bar.
What it means: When you see a STRONG badge, every single model agrees the line is badly mispriced. These are the highest-conviction bets in the system.
Verified backtest (OOS 2023-2025, odds confirmed ~2h pre-game via Odds API):
  112 bets · 59.8% win · +46.3% ROI · +51.8u · Sharpe 5.11
  2023: +39% · 2024: +69% · 2025: +32% — positive all 3 years, highest ROI per bet
PRIME / STANDARD
PRIME rule: V1's LR+LGBM both agree on away edge ≥10% (with <6% spread), AND V2's LR model independently sees ≥12% away edge. This uses V2's logistic regression specifically — V2's LGBM may disagree, but the LR-only gate filters LGBM noise and catches high-value games that don't qualify as STRONG.
STANDARD rule: Both V1 and V2 model generations independently flag the same game on the same side at ≥10% edge — but the game did NOT qualify as STRONG or PRIME.
What it means: Two completely independent model pipelines — different features, different training data, different regularization — both see the same mispricing. When V1 and V2 disagree, single-model flags are essentially worthless (breakeven or negative ROI).
Verified backtest (OOS 2023-2025, odds confirmed ~2h pre-game via Odds API):
  PRIME (exclusive): ~56 bets · +38.3% ROI · Sharpe 4.50
    2023: +44% · 2024: +45% · 2025: +28% — positive all 3 years
  STANDARD (exclusive): ~36 bets · +31.6% ROI · Sharpe 3.66
    2023: +27% · 2024: +41% · 2025: +26% — positive all 3 years

For comparison — single-model-only flags (no consensus):
  V1 ONLY (V2 doesn't flag): 215 bets · 47.0% win · +1.4% ROI — marginal at best
  V2 ONLY (V1 doesn't flag): 169 bets · 40.8% win · -4.4% ROI — losing
The gap between consensus and single-model is enormous. Only bet consensus.
NARROW EDGE — Home Consensus (0.5u)
Rule: Both V1 and V2 independently agree the home team is undervalued by ≥5%, AND at least one signal gate condition is met: visiting bullpen is fatigued (fatigue gap > 0) OR pitcher-friendly park (park factor < median). V1 model spread must be ≤6%. V2 model spread filter is NOT applied (counterproductive for home bets — removing it improves CI from crossing zero to solidly above). Purple badge. Half-unit (0.5u) sizing.
Why the signal gate: Raw consensus alone (no gate) has a CI crossing zero. Analysis of 4 confirming signals found that "fatigue OR low park" separates profitable home bets from unprofitable ones. Both conditions have clear baseball logic — you're betting home teams against a tired bullpen or in a run-suppressing park.
Verified OOS (2023-2025) — using real pre-game odds, post-DH dedup (10,000 bootstrap):
  HOME standalone: 193 bets · 53.9% win · +18.9% ROI · +36.4u · P(profitable): 98.8%
    2023: 81 bets · +10% ROI
    2024: 54 bets · +6% ROI
    2025: 58 bets · +43% ROI
    All three OOS years profitable.
  Bootstrap 95% CI: [+2.5%, +34.9%] — clears zero
  Zero overlap with away consensus (different bet side)
  ★ STRONG SPOT badge when ≥3 of 4 confirming signals fire (informational — CI crosses zero at this subsample size)

Full system (1u away + 0.5u home):
  397 bets · +30.6% ROI · +92.0u · Sharpe 3.66 · P(profitable): 100.0%
  HOME adds 193 bets (+95% volume) and +18.2u (+25% profit) vs away-only

Recommended sizing: Half unit (0.5u) per bet. CI clears zero [+2.5%, +34.9%] but half-unit remains prudent for a signal-gated tier. Can be promoted to full unit after live validation.
Risk profile: Stays profitable with 75% edge degradation (+4.7% ROI). Maximum yearly exposure: ~32u at 0.5u stakes.
Dashboard Terms
Model (4-Blend)
Home win probability averaged across all four models (V1 LR, V1 LGBM, V2 LR, V2 LGBM). This is the best-calibrated probability available (Brier 0.2438). Shown in the game table and on bet cards.
Edge
Gap between the 4-blend model probability and Vegas implied probability. Larger = model disagrees more with the market. Positive = model favors that side more than Vegas does.
Strong
Highest conviction. All 4 sub-models agree on ≥12% away edge. Gold badge. Best per-bet returns (+46.3% verified ROI, Sharpe 5.11, positive all 3 OOS years).
Prime
V1 flags STANDARD (≥10% edge) and V2's logistic regression independently sees ≥12% edge, but not all 4 models clear 12%. Cyan badge. Best risk-adjusted total returns (+38.3% verified ROI, positive all 3 OOS years).
Standard
Both V1 and V2 model generations independently flag the same game on the same side at ≥10% edge, but not STRONG or PRIME. Cyan badge. When only one generation flags, the bet is NOT recommended.
Narrow Edge
Both V1 and V2 agree the home team is undervalued by ≥5%, PLUS a signal gate: visiting bullpen is fatigued OR pitcher-friendly park. Purple badge. Half-unit (0.5u) sizing. Verified OOS: 193 bets, +18.9% ROI, CI [+2.5%, +34.9%], P(profitable) 98.8%, all three years positive. Games with ≥3 confirming signals get a ★ STRONG SPOT badge (informational). Full system: 397 bets, +30.6% ROI, Sharpe 3.66.
Vegas Implied
Home win probability derived from the median moneyline across sportsbooks (FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Bet365, Caesars).
Best Odds
The sportsbook currently offering the best moneyline for the flagged side. Always bet at the best available price. Backtest ROI is calculated using the best line available ~2h pre-game — the same window used by the live dashboard.
Confirming & Warning Signals — Away Bets
Green and orange badges appear on flagged away consensus bets based on game-context patterns. These don't change the bet recommendation — they indicate when conditions are especially favorable or unfavorable. Stats below are validated on the away consensus subset (OOS 2023-2025).
Game 3+ of series
Edge is dramatically stronger later in a series, likely driven by compounding bullpen fatigue and lineup familiarity. Consensus OOS: STRONG +80.4% ROI (40 bets, 75% win), STANDARD +56.6% (65 bets, 66% win). The single strongest confirming signal.
Disciplined opponent
Home lineup has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). Counterintuitively, edge is strongest against disciplined teams. Consensus OOS: STRONG +67.4% ROI (66 bets, 67% win), STANDARD +43.2% (106 bets, 59% win).
Fatigued opposing bullpen
Home bullpen worked more innings recently than away bullpen (fatigue gap > 0). Consensus OOS: STRONG +52.2% ROI (59 bets) vs +44.2% when not fatigued. STANDARD +40.3% (102 bets) vs +26.8%.
Game 2 caution
Game 2 of a series is the weakest spot. Consensus OOS: STRONG +8.6% ROI (37 bets, 43% win), STANDARD +1.0% (63 bets, 43% win). Still marginally positive on consensus bets but much weaker than Game 1 or Game 3+. Exercise caution or reduce sizing.
Undisciplined opponent
Home lineup has above-median chase rate (less disciplined). Consensus OOS: STRONG +22.6% ROI (49 bets), STANDARD +22.8% (92 bets). Still profitable on consensus bets (unlike single-generation-only flags), but roughly half the ROI of disciplined-opponent bets.
September
September is the weakest month, likely due to roster expansion and callups. Consensus OOS: STRONG +49.6% ROI (18 bets, 56% win), STANDARD +27.6% (37 bets, 51% win). Still positive post-dedup but smaller sample — use caution.
Confirming & Warning Signals — Home Bets
Home consensus bets have fundamentally different patterns than away bets — several signals are inverted. These are validated on the home consensus subset (193 bets, verified OOS odds). Away signals should NOT be applied to home bets. Stats below are directional — bullpen fatigue and low park factor are used as the signal gate filter.
Bullpen fatigue edge
When the home bullpen is more fatigued than the away bullpen (fatigue gap > 0), home consensus ROI is strongest. This is the opposite of away bets where opposing fatigue helps. Home OOS: +140.7% ROI (115 bets, 52% win) vs -5.3% when not fatigued. The single strongest home confirming signal.
HR-prone opposing starter
Away starter has above-median HR/FB rate — more vulnerable to home run power. Home OOS: +106.1% ROI (124 bets, 49% win) vs +17.5% when away starter has low HR/FB.
Pitcher-friendly park
Low park factor (< median ~0.99) — counterintuitively, home consensus edges are strongest in pitcher parks. Home OOS: +106.9% ROI (151 bets, 52% win) vs -6.8% in hitter-friendly parks.
Disciplined home lineup
Home team has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). This signal transfers from away bets. Home OOS: +95.5% ROI (127 bets, 46% win) vs +26.1% when undisciplined.
Game 3+ (weaker for home)
Unlike away bets where Game 3+ is the strongest spot, home consensus weakens later in a series. Home OOS: +25.0% ROI (95 bets, 47% win) vs +82.2% in Games 1-2. The pattern is inverted from away.
September
September is weak for both home and away bets. Home OOS: +6.3% ROI (61 bets, 54% win) vs +78.0% non-September. Marginally positive but much thinner edge.
Hitter-friendly park
High park factor (> ~1.04) erodes home consensus edge. Home OOS: -6.8% ROI (103 bets, 46% win). The only signal that produces negative ROI for home bets.
Roster sensitivity: Home consensus bets are 1.45x more sensitive to lineup changes than away bets. The dashboard requires at least "projected" roster confidence for home bets — "starter only" or "unknown" rosters will not trigger home consensus flags. When projected, verify the lineup before placing.
Batting & Lineup Stats
wRC+
Weighted Runs Created Plus. Total offensive value adjusted for park and league. 100 = average; 120 = 20% above average.
wOBA
Weighted On-Base Average. Weights each outcome (single, double, HR, walk) by its actual run value. League average ~.310-.320.
Chase Rate
Percentage of pitches outside the strike zone a batter swings at. Lower = more disciplined.
Lineup K Rate
How often a team's batters strike out. Lower = more contact, more balls in play.
Top 3 wOBA
Combined wOBA of a team's top 3 lineup spots — the strength of the heart of the order.
Pitching Stats
xFIP
Expected Fielding Independent Pitching. Estimates run prevention independent of defense and batted-ball luck. Uses expected HR rate. Lower = better. League avg ~4.00.
ERA
Earned Run Average. Earned runs allowed per 9 innings. Lower = better. More volatile than xFIP because it includes defense and sequencing.
Stuff+
Pitch Stuff Plus. Raw quality of a pitcher's arsenal — velocity, movement, spin. 100 = average; 120+ = elite.
K Rate
Percentage of plate appearances ending in a strikeout. Higher = more dominant pitching.
Bullpen ERA
Collective ERA of a team's relief pitchers. Matters late in games when starters exit.
Team & Context Stats
Pythagorean Win%
Expected win percentage based on runs scored vs allowed over the last 60 days. Captures true team strength better than W-L record.
Run Differential
Runs scored minus runs allowed (60-day window). Positive = outscoring opponents.
Park Factor
How much a ballpark inflates or deflates run scoring. 1.00 = neutral; Coors Field ~1.30 (30% more runs).
Betting Terms
Edge
Gap between the model's predicted probability and Vegas implied probability. Larger = model disagrees more with the market.
ROI
Return on Investment. Profit / total risked. +20% means $20 profit per $100 wagered.
Units (u)
Standardized bet size. 1u = your chosen base wager. All bets are flat 1u regardless of edge size or odds.
Moneyline
Negative = risk that much to win $100 (-150 = risk $150 to win $100). Positive = profit on $100 bet (+130 = $130 profit).
Closing Line Value
Difference between the odds you bet and the final odds at game time. Consistently beating the close is the strongest sign of a profitable bettor.
OOS
Out-of-Sample. Data the model never saw during training. Our models train on 2001-2022; seasons 2023-2025 are OOS and represent real predictive performance.
