Glossary & Methodology
How This Model Works
Three independent model generations — V1, V2, and V3 — each run LR + LGBM pairs to predict home win probability for every MLB game.

V1: LR (C=0.5) + LGBM · 36 features · trained through 2022
V2: LR (C=0.001) + LGBM · 35 features (pruned from 98) · trained through 2021
V3: LR (C=0.001) + LGBM · 35 features · walk-forward retrained through 2025

Display probability: 6-blend (equal weight V1/V2/V3 LR+LGBM), calibration slope 1.007 — the most accurate probability available.

The three generations use different feature sets, regularization, and training windows — making them genuinely independent. Multi-model agreement dramatically outperforms single-model flags. All performance figures below use modal de-vigged odds computed consistently across all tiers.

Odds validation: All backtest ROI figures use verified pre-game odds from The Odds API historical endpoint. Best available line across sportsbooks, matching the line-shopping approach used for live betting.

Confirming & Warning Signals — Away Bets
Green and orange badges appear on flagged away bets based on game-context patterns. These don't change the bet recommendation — they indicate when conditions are especially favorable or unfavorable. Stats below are validated on the away tier subset (OOS 2023-2025).
Game 3+ of series
Edge is dramatically stronger later in a series, likely driven by compounding bullpen fatigue and lineup familiarity. OOS: STRONG +80.4% ROI (40 bets, 75% win), STANDARD +56.6% (65 bets, 66% win). The single strongest confirming signal.
Disciplined opponent
Home lineup has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). Counterintuitively, edge is strongest against disciplined teams. OOS: STRONG +67.4% ROI (66 bets, 67% win), STANDARD +43.2% (106 bets, 59% win).
Fatigued opposing bullpen
Home bullpen worked more innings recently than away bullpen (fatigue gap > 0). OOS: STRONG +52.2% ROI (59 bets) vs +44.2% when not fatigued. STANDARD +40.3% (102 bets) vs +26.8%.
Game 2 caution
Game 2 of a series is the weakest spot. OOS: STRONG +8.6% ROI (37 bets, 43% win), STANDARD +1.0% (63 bets, 43% win). Still marginally positive but much weaker than Game 1 or Game 3+. Exercise caution or reduce sizing.
Undisciplined opponent
Home lineup has above-median chase rate (less disciplined). OOS: STRONG +22.6% ROI (49 bets), STANDARD +22.8% (92 bets). Still profitable (unlike single-generation-only flags), but roughly half the ROI of disciplined-opponent bets.
September
September is the weakest month, likely due to roster expansion and callups. OOS: STRONG +49.6% ROI (18 bets, 56% win), STANDARD +27.6% (37 bets, 51% win). Still positive but smaller sample — use caution.
Confirming & Warning Signals — Narrow Edge (Home Bets)
Narrow Edge bets have fundamentally different patterns than away bets — several signals are inverted. These are validated on the Narrow Edge subset (193 bets, verified OOS odds). Away signals should NOT be applied to Narrow Edge bets. Stats below are directional — bullpen fatigue and low park factor are used as the signal gate filter.
Bullpen fatigue edge
When the opposing bullpen is fatigued (fatigue gap > 0), Narrow Edge ROI is strongest. This is the opposite of away bets where opposing fatigue helps. Narrow Edge OOS: +140.7% ROI (115 bets, 52% win) vs -5.3% when not fatigued. The single strongest confirming signal.
HR-prone opposing starter
Away starter has above-median HR/FB rate — more vulnerable to home run power. Narrow Edge OOS: +106.1% ROI (124 bets, 49% win) vs +17.5% when away starter has low HR/FB.
Pitcher-friendly park
Low park factor (< median ~0.99) — counterintuitively, Narrow Edge bets are strongest in pitcher parks. Narrow Edge OOS: +106.9% ROI (151 bets, 52% win) vs -6.8% in hitter-friendly parks.
Disciplined home lineup
Home team has below-median chase rate (more disciplined). This signal transfers from away bets. Narrow Edge OOS: +95.5% ROI (127 bets, 46% win) vs +26.1% when undisciplined.
Game 3+ (weaker for Narrow Edge)
Unlike away bets where Game 3+ is the strongest spot, Narrow Edge bets weaken later in a series. Narrow Edge OOS: +25.0% ROI (95 bets, 47% win) vs +82.2% in Games 1-2. The pattern is inverted from away.
September
September is weak for both home and away bets. Narrow Edge OOS: +6.3% ROI (61 bets, 54% win) vs +78.0% non-September. Marginally positive but much thinner edge.
Hitter-friendly park
High park factor (> ~1.04) erodes the Narrow Edge. Narrow Edge OOS: -6.8% ROI (103 bets, 46% win). The only signal that produces negative ROI.
Roster sensitivity: Narrow Edge bets are 1.45x more sensitive to lineup changes than away bets. The dashboard requires at least "projected" roster confidence — "starter only" or "unknown" rosters will not trigger Narrow Edge flags. When projected, verify the lineup before placing.
Batting & Lineup Stats
wRC+
Weighted Runs Created Plus. Total offensive value adjusted for park and league. 100 = average; 120 = 20% above average.
wOBA
Weighted On-Base Average. Weights each outcome (single, double, HR, walk) by its actual run value. League average ~.310-.320.
Chase Rate
Percentage of pitches outside the strike zone a batter swings at. Lower = more disciplined.
Lineup K Rate
How often a team's batters strike out. Lower = more contact, more balls in play.
Top 3 wOBA
Combined wOBA of a team's top 3 lineup spots — the strength of the heart of the order.
Pitching Stats
xFIP
Expected Fielding Independent Pitching. Estimates run prevention independent of defense and batted-ball luck. Uses expected HR rate. Lower = better. League avg ~4.00.
ERA
Earned Run Average. Earned runs allowed per 9 innings. Lower = better. More volatile than xFIP because it includes defense and sequencing.
Stuff+
Pitch Stuff Plus. Raw quality of a pitcher's arsenal — velocity, movement, spin. 100 = average; 120+ = elite.
K Rate
Percentage of plate appearances ending in a strikeout. Higher = more dominant pitching.
Bullpen ERA
Collective ERA of a team's relief pitchers. Matters late in games when starters exit.
Team & Context Stats
Pythagorean Win%
Expected win percentage based on runs scored vs allowed over the last 60 days. Captures true team strength better than W-L record.
Run Differential
Runs scored minus runs allowed (60-day window). Positive = outscoring opponents.
Park Factor
How much a ballpark inflates or deflates run scoring. 1.00 = neutral; Coors Field ~1.30 (30% more runs).
Betting Terms
Edge
Gap between the model's predicted probability and Vegas implied probability. Larger = model disagrees more with the market.
ROI
Return on Investment. Profit / total risked. +20% means $20 profit per $100 wagered.
Units (u)
Standardized bet size. 1u = your chosen base wager. All bets are flat 1u regardless of edge size or odds.
Moneyline
Negative = risk that much to win $100 (-150 = risk $150 to win $100). Positive = profit on $100 bet (+130 = $130 profit).
Closing Line Value
Difference between the odds you bet and the final odds at game time. Consistently beating the close is the strongest sign of a profitable bettor.
OOS
Out-of-Sample. Data the model never saw during training. Our models train on 2001-2022; seasons 2023-2025 are OOS and represent real predictive performance.
